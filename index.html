03 / Artist Statement

“Shadow is not the absence of light, but the architecture of memory.”

Founded as an independent visual archive, Marked Noir strips away chromatic distraction to reveal raw geometry, tension, and human presence. Working predominantly with medium-format film and dedicated monochrome sensors, every frame is sculpted with deliberate restraint.
